About Veldra

Started by mechanics, still run by people who fix things

Veldra was founded in 1997 by two agricultural mechanics who were tired of selling machinery they couldn't stand behind. Nearly three decades later, that's still the filter every product and service passes through: would we put this on our own land?

Today our team covers equipment sales, field engineering, parts logistics and workshop servicing — all under one roof, so a farm never has to juggle three different suppliers for one machine.
